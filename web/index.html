AI-assisted agreement monitoring

DealEscrow

A research platform for creating, reviewing, accepting and monitoring conditional financial agreements between two parties.

Document AI

PDF, DOCX, OCR and written-comment ingestion with structured term extraction and confidence review.

Agreement Engine

Versioned terms, document hashes and explicit acceptance from both parties.

Finance Engine

Deterministic interest, grace-period, due-date and balance calculations.

Automation

n8n workflows for reminders, monitoring, human review and authorized provider actions.

Compliance

Mandate checks, data minimisation and review gates for consequential legal or payment actions.

Auditability

Structured event history for research, debugging and transparent dispute review.

Research prototype. Not a bank, payment institution, law firm or autonomous legal decision-maker.
